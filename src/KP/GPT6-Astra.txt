Theodore: Fair Juliet, why dost thou weep beneath the moon?
What sorrow hath stolen the sweetness from thy face,
That even the stars seem dimmed by grief?

Juliet: Alas, dear Theodore, my heart is heavy with woe.
The night is long, the winds lament,
And every whispered breeze doth speak thy name.

Theodore: Then let my words be balm unto thy sorrow;
For though the world should turn against us,
I shall remain thy shelter in the storm.

Juliet: Thy kindness shines brighter than the morning star.
With thee, I fear no darkness, nor death itself.

Theodore: Come, let us flee this cruel night together,
And write our fate where none may command it.
